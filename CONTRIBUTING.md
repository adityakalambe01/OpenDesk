We welcome contributions from the global open-source community to make OpenDesk the most secure, performant, and reliable remote desktop platform.

Please review our complete contribution instructions, development setup guide, and coding style rules in our official documentation:

- 📖 **[Contribution Guidelines & Workflow](docs/06-development/CONTRIBUTING.md)**
- 💻 **[Local Development Setup Guide](docs/06-development/DEVELOPMENT_SETUP.md)**
- 🎨 **[Engineering Standards & Style Guide](docs/06-development/CODING_STANDARDS.md)**
- 🌿 **[Branching Strategy & PR Requirements](docs/06-development/BRANCHING_STRATEGY.md)**
- 🧪 **[Automated Testing Strategy](docs/06-development/TESTING_STRATEGY.md)**
- 📋 **[Master Task Checklist (MASTER_TODO.md)](docs/08-tasks/MASTER_TODO.md)**

All contributions must adhere to our [Code of Conduct](docs/06-development/CONTRIBUTING.md#1-code-of-conduct) and require a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-off (`git commit -s`).
